## 🌿 About AYYAN SNACKS SPOT

**AYYAN SNACKS SPOT** is a traditional South Indian snacks business
serving authentic and wholesome food since 2000.

This application is designed to create a **simple digital connection between
the business and its customers**.

It brings the traditional AYYAN experience into the digital world while
keeping the interaction simple, warm and personal.

## 📩 Customer Connection

The application provides a dedicated **customer query system**.

Customers can share:

- **Name**
- **Contact Number**
- **Email Address**
- **Message**

This creates a direct communication channel between the customer
and AYYAN SNACKS SPOT.

After submitting a query, the customer receives a confirmation email.
