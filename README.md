🌿 A Living Tradition, Connected Digitally

AYYAN SNACKS SPOT is a traditional South Indian snacks business serving authentic and wholesome food since 2000.

This web application brings that traditional identity into a modern digital experience — making it easier for customers to discover the food, understand the story, explore the menu and communicate directly with the business.
